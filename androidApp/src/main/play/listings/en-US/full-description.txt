Remindly is a simple daily reminder app for the things that matter, from medication on schedule to everyday tasks.

Set a pill reminder after breakfast or at an exact time, add personal reminders that repeat every day, on workdays, every few days or once a month, and get a notification right when you need it. Turn on alarm mode for reminders you really can't miss.

Use Remindly as:

• A medication reminder and pill tracker for your daily routine
• Everyday reminders for appointments and important dates
• Pet care and grooming reminders
• Reminders for meter readings and regular household tasks
• Anything else you don't want to forget

No complicated task manager, no overloaded planner. Just reminders that arrive on time.

Stay on schedule. Remember what matters.
